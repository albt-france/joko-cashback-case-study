# Assumptions of the SIMULATED data

Everything in `data/simulated/` is **SIMULATED**. It is generated by `src/simulate.py` (fixed seed 42) and is **not Joko data**.
It exists to demonstrate metric definitions, a dashboard, a retention model and an AI assistant on a realistic shape of data.

## What is real inside the simulation
- **Merchants and their categories**: real merchant names and categories from the public Widilo/iGraal snapshot (`data/public/dim_merchant.csv`), up to 300 merchants.
- **Cashback rates**: each simulated purchase earns the merchant's **median public percentage rate** (PUBLIC DATA, France, 11 Nov 2026).

## Invented assumptions
| Topic | Assumption |
|---|---|
| Period | 2025-05-01 to 2026-10-31 (18 months) |
| Users | 30,000 sign-ups; volume grows over time and follows the monthly seasonality below |
| Country | 5% US before Sept 2026, 30% US from Sept 2026 (assumed US launch); the rest France |
| Channels | organic 30%, referral 25%, paid social 20%, app-store search 15%, influencer 10% |
| Channel engagement multiplier | referral 1.25, organic 1.0, app-store search 0.95, influencer 0.85, paid social 0.75 |
| Platforms | iOS 55%, Android 35%, browser extension 10% (no platform effect is simulated) |
| Engagement | each user has a latent engagement drawn from a log-normal (sigma 0.6) × channel multiplier |
| Weekly activity | sessions ~ Poisson(1.6 × engagement), searches ~ 0.8 per session, merchant clicks ~ 0.35 per session × seasonality |
| Purchases | Poisson(0.16 × engagement × seasonality × assistant effect) per active week; basket log-normal, median ~ EUR 55 |
| Seasonality | Jan 1.25 (sales), Jul 1.2 (sales), Nov 1.7 (Black Friday), Dec 1.4, Aug 0.85, other months 0.9-1.0 |
| Validation delay | travel: normal(75, 25) days; other categories: normal(38, 14) days; minimum 3 days |
| Status | 9% of purchases cancelled; purchases whose validation date is after the data end are `pending` |
| Payouts | when the validated balance reaches EUR 10, the user cashes out with probability 0.7 |
| **Churn** (weekly hazard) | 0.11 / engagement |
| **Mechanism 1 - activation** | from week 2, users with a purchase in their first 14 days have their churn hazard × 0.45 |
| **Mechanism 2 - waiting** | if the user's first cashback takes > 45 days to validate, churn hazard × 2.0 once they have waited 5+ weeks |
| **Mechanism 3 - seasonality** | see above |
| **Mechanism 4 - AI assistant** | adoption probability = logistic(1.8 × ln(engagement) − 0.9) → engaged users adopt more (**selection bias**). True causal effect on purchase frequency: **+10%** from the adoption week |

## Why these mechanisms
They encode the hypotheses a cashback analyst would want to test on real data: activation drives retention; long validation delays
erode trust (a theme visible in public reviews, page 5); demand is seasonal; and the effect of an AI assistant is easy to overstate
when adopters are compared with non-adopters (page 9 / notebook section 4 shows the naive estimate vs the known truth).
